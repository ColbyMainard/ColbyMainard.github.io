Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 29, 2026

This page explains what data colbymainard.github.io collects, why, and how to contact the site owner with questions or removal requests. This site is a personal portfolio operated by Colby Mainard and is hosted on GitHub Pages. It collects no personal information directly — everything described below is collected by third-party services that are loaded into the page so visitor analytics and search-engine eligibility can be measured.

What Google Analytics collects

This site can load Google Analytics 4, but only after you accept the cookie banner shown on your first visit. If you reject the banner, or simply close it without choosing, the Google Analytics scripts are never requested and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your decision at any time in the Cookie preferences section below.

When you do accept, Google Analytics collects the following information about each visit:

  • Your IP address (Google truncates the last octet before storage to anonymize it).
  • The page URL and page title you viewed.
  • The referring URL (the page you arrived from), if any.
  • Your browser, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address (city / region / country).
  • Session duration, the order of pages you viewed, and whether you are a new or returning visitor.
  • Standard click and scroll events the GA4 default configuration captures.

The site owner uses this data only to understand which content is read, how visitors arrive, and roughly where they are coming from. The data is retained inside Google's systems for 14 months and then automatically deleted; it is never sold or shared with anyone.

Google Analytics 4 sets one or more cookies in your browser (typically _ga and _ga_<measurement-id>) so it can distinguish unique visitors and sessions. If you would prefer not to be measured, you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, block third-party cookies, or use any browser extension that blocks googletagmanager.com — the rest of this site continues to work normally.

Cookie preferences

On your first visit a banner is shown at the bottom of every page asking whether you want to accept analytics cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key cm-cookie-consent; the banner does not reappear on later visits unless you reset that choice below.

Use these controls to change your decision. Reject and Reset stop Google Analytics from loading on subsequent page loads, but they do not delete cookies that were already placed in your browser before you opted out — clear those via your browser's site-data controls or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Search-engine verification meta tags

The home page (index.html) carries two verification <meta> tags so this site can be associated with the owner's accounts at the major search engines:

  • google-site-verification — lets the site owner access this domain inside Google Search Console (indexing status, search-impression data, indexing errors).
  • msvalidate.01 — the Bing equivalent, used with Bing Webmaster Tools (provided by Microsoft).

These tags are inert beacons. They do not load any script, set any cookie, or transmit any data on their own — they exist purely so the search engines can confirm the site owner controls this domain when the owner separately logs in to those tools. They do, however, indicate to those services that this site exists.

Service worker (offline cache)

When you visit any page on this site over HTTPS, a service worker registers in your browser and stores copies of the site's HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and images in your browser's local cache. This is so the pages load quickly on repeat visits and remain readable if your connection drops. The cache lives entirely in your browser; nothing is sent off-device. You can clear it at any time via your browser's site data / cache controls.

Third-party content embedded on this site

A few pages embed content hosted by third parties. When your browser loads such embeds it contacts those third parties directly, which means they receive your IP address and may set their own cookies independent of this site:

  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) — the Hobbies → Quantum Computing section embeds a video via youtube-nocookie.com, which is YouTube's reduced-tracking variant. YouTube only sets cookies if you start playing the video. See the Google / YouTube privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr CDN — AnimeJS is loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net on every animated page. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.
  • External links to other sites (e.g. links to books on Goodreads, articles on Wikipedia, podcasts on Spotify) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies; this site has no visibility into what they collect.

Your rights and how to exercise them

Depending on where you live, you may have the right underGDPR (European Economic Area / United Kingdom) or the CCPA (California) to request a copy of any personal data this site holds about you, to ask for it to be deleted, or to opt out of its sale. This site does not sell personal data and does not retain it server-side; the only data associated with your visit is held by the third parties listed above (primarily Google Analytics).

To make a deletion or access request that this site can act on, contact the site owner using the email address in the footer below. For requests about data held by Google Analytics specifically, you can also use Google's privacy controls directly — Google can act on those without involving the site owner.

Changes to this policy

If the data this site collects changes (for example, a new analytics provider is added or removed, or new third-party content is embedded) the date at the top of this page will be updated and the policy text will reflect the change. There is no email notification list; check back here if you want to confirm the current state.
