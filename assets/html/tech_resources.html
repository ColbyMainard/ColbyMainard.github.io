Curated AI, Cybersecurity & Programming Learning Resources

Most of us are curious about the technology we use every day.

However, it is not always obvious where to start when it comes to being informed.

Below is a curated list of AI/ML, cybersecurity, and programming resources I put together, categorized by subject. I purposefully limited the podcasts and book recommendations to content I either listen to, have read, or own to ensure quality. Certifications lists were selected largely based on anecdotes and feedback from those in the relevant industry, as well as the relevant podcasts listed.

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Cybersecurity

New to the field? Start with my beginner cybersecurity guide, then use the lists below to go deeper.

Certifications

Cybersecurity certifications
Name Description
Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) Assesses knowledge across eight domains of information security, and requires five years of experience in cybersecurity.
Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) Best for those working in governance of IT systems.
CompTIA Security+ A great way to demonstrate core security knowledge for those new in the industry.
Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) Teaches how to think like a hacker and defend against real-world cyber threats.
Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) Mainly focused on making sure managers can apply proper governance and risk assessment.
